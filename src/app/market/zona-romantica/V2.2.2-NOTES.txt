SearchPV IMD V2.2.2

Focused cockpit/animation correction from V2.2.1.

- Desktop cockpit is now 3 columns x 2 rows and substantially more compact.
- Dark cockpit Market Snapshot reel glyphs remain champagne/gold during the spin.
- Current Inventory no longer animates SVG stroke-dash length. The complete correct line is rendered and revealed left-to-right with a clip-path, avoiding failures at sharp MLS inventory discontinuities such as OFRENDA.
- The reveal applies to both compact cockpit and full analytical Current Inventory views.
- No API or market-data calculation changes.
