# Project Atlas
## Roadmap

---

## Philosophy

Atlas will be built in layers.

Each phase should produce a usable, more beautiful, and more capable application.

The goal is continuous evolution rather than large releases.

At every milestone, Atlas should remain functional and enjoyable to use.

---

# Phase 1
## Foundation

Objective:

Create the permanent architecture of Atlas.

Deliverables:

- Atlas Viewport
- Atlas Overlay
- Atlas Search
- Atlas Bottom Sheet
- Atlas State
- Camera Controller
- Entity Service
- Basic navigation

Success:

Atlas can navigate to any canonical geographic entity.

---

# Phase 2
## Geographic Exploration

Objective:

Allow users to naturally explore the region.

Deliverables:

- Geographic hierarchy
- Zones
- Areas
- Communities
- Developments
- Beaches
- Search integration
- Camera transitions

Success:

Users can discover places without searching for listings.

---

# Phase 3
## Geographic Intelligence

Objective:

Turn geography into information.

Deliverables:

- Community descriptions
- Photography
- Nearby places
- Walkability
- Beach access
- Parks
- Restaurants
- Coffee
- Medical
- Schools
- Golf
- Marinas

Success:

Atlas becomes the best way to learn about a place.

---

# Phase 4
## Market Intelligence

Objective:

Integrate SearchPV's market intelligence directly into Atlas.

Deliverables:

- Inventory
- Closed sales
- Pending sales
- Median price
- Price per square meter
- Days on market
- Historical charts
- Market trends

Success:

Every place has a living market profile.

---

# Phase 5
## Property Discovery

Objective:

Connect geography with listings.

Deliverables:

- Search Properties
- Property markers
- Development pages
- Listing previews
- Property comparisons
- Saved searches

Success:

Users naturally transition from learning about a place to discovering properties.

---

# Phase 6
## Intelligent Geography

Objective:

Introduce conversational interaction.

Deliverables:

Natural language examples:

"Show me beachfront condos under $700,000."

"Compare Marina Vallarta and Bucerías."

"Which communities appreciated the most last year?"

Atlas responds by:

- moving the camera
- selecting places
- updating the workspace
- presenting market intelligence

Success:

AI becomes another way to explore geography rather than another search box.

---

# Phase 7
## Living Atlas

Objective:

Create the definitive exploration platform for Puerto Vallarta and Riviera Nayarit.

Potential capabilities:

- Personalized recommendations
- Saved journeys
- Travel itineraries
- Market alerts
- Investment analysis
- Lifestyle matching
- Interactive storytelling
- Future geographic datasets

Atlas continues to evolve without changing its underlying architecture.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Some parts of Atlas will mature continuously.

These include:

- Boundary quality
- Photography
- Nearby places
- Community descriptions
- Market analytics
- AI capabilities

The platform should improve naturally as these datasets grow.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every new feature should satisfy three questions:

1. Does it help users understand a place?

2. Does it integrate naturally into Atlas State?

3. Does it make the experience more elegant rather than more complicated?

If the answer to any of these is "no," reconsider the design before implementing it.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Atlas is not simply another interface for SearchPV.

It becomes the primary way users experience Puerto Vallarta real estate.

Search, geography, market intelligence, AI, and listings all become different perspectives on the same geographic world.

The map remains constant.

The user's understanding continues to grow.
